Diagnosis

Before redesigning, I looked at 30 days of real visitor data. Most people use a desktop, not a phone - so that's the focus here.

66%

Most visitors stop scrolling halfway down the page. They never even see the plans.

Scroll depth, all sessions
0%

Not one of the 101 people who got stuck on the table was just browsing. Most (65) were highly interested buyers.

User intent, dead-click sessions only (101 sessions)
92%

9 out of 10 visits happen on a desktop, not a phone. So the redesign is built for desktop first.

Devices, all sessions
36%

More than a third of people's attention stays near the very top of the page.

Attention heatmap, all sessions
91%

9 in 10 of the top traffic sources are Wiley's own pages, not a search engine.

Top referrers, all sessions

At a glance

Two quick summaries of the challenge and the goal.

The problem

Comparing our 3 editing plans already took effort. Post-AI Editing, our 4th service, isn't directly comparable to the others - it doesn't fit the same format. That meant restructuring the section, not just adding one more option.

The goal

Make all 4 plans easy to scan - in seconds, not minutes.
