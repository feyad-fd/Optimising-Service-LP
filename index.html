Diagnosis

Service Level 2 is where authors pick a service. If it is unclear, they leave.

We are introducing Post-AI Editing as a new addition to our existing service portfolio.

  • 01

    Services are hard to scan and compare.

  • 02

    A 4th service, Post-AI Editing, isn't directly comparable to the others - it doesn't fit the same format.

  • 03

    The tabular format is very text heavy.

How did the design change?

Design Decisions

Goal: Let users get what they want without confusing them.

  • 01

    Show only necessary information in front (Progressive Disclosure)

  • 02

    Surface prices upfront, reducing one click and possible dropoffs due to frustration.

  • 03

    Refine copy to make it easy to understand for users.

  • 04

    Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) followed

Impact

Better information presentation on services offered: $22.3K incremental revenue in Q2.
