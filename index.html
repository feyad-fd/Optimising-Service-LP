Context

What this page does?

The company helps researchers polish papers before they submit to journals. This service page is where people pick a service: Standard, Advanced, or Scientific Editing. We're introducing a 4th - Post-AI Editing, or papers drafted with AI tools and rebranding the page with client facing names for services.

At a glance

Two quick summaries of the challenge and the goal.

The problem

Comparing our 3 editing plans already took effort. Post-AI Editing, our 4th service, isn't directly comparable to the others - it doesn't fit the same format. That meant restructuring the section, not just adding one more option.

The goal

Make all 4 plans easy to scan - in seconds, not minutes.
